VISITORS AT THE WINDOW / 窗外有客 — Compositor Study 02

3200 × 2000 px / sRGB / 144 ppi

Two oversized birds visit a real old-town window. The yellow bird sits on its photographed branch above the red bird peeking from below. Roofs establish their impossible scale; the foreground window occludes both birds and the branch ends.

Editable structure — 19 layers in five folders:
Outside — original town photograph and cool daylight curve
Visitor 2 — full yellow-bird source, editable bird/feet/perch mask and daylight curve
Visitor 1 — full red-bird source, user-refined mask/rotation/placement and two curves
Window — foreground window photograph, user-refined opening/slit mask and room curve
Typography — five editable text layers using Baskerville and Helvetica

The project embeds its source pixels and masks and opens without the Assets folder. Hide Typography for artwork without lettering. Select an image thumbnail to transform it; select its mask thumbnail to refine its edges. Save the .comp before checking Git: export alone does not save project edits.

The window source was reduced to 4096 × 3072 for the embedded layers; both bird sources retain their original 2880 × 3600 pixels. The red bird retains the user's -21° rotation and approximately 101.7% scale; the yellow bird retains the user's approximately 45.3% scale and placement.

The yellow bird's starting mask uses local Apple Vision foreground selection, supplemented with the photographed perch and claws. The background between the legs is removed; edges are slightly contracted. All scene pixels come from the three supplied photographs. The generated concept sketch is not a project layer.
