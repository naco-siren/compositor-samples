BLOOM / 花人 — Compositor Study 02

3200 × 2000 px / sRGB / 144 ppi

A blue, white and burgundy bouquet replaces the head of a figure wrapped in a red patterned shawl. The photographed garment overlaps the flower stems; its original hand remains visible. The old-house photograph forms a rectangular backdrop, while petals and the figure extend beyond its boundary.

20 editable layers in five folders:
Ground — ivory-to-jade background
Setting — old-house photograph, panel mask and softer-daylight curve
Flowers — masked bouquet, local perimeter color cleanup, warmer-whites curve, and a hidden untouched original bouquet
Portrait — full original portrait with a garment/hand mask, textile curve and a masked collar shade
Typography — six editable text layers, including Study 02 and page 02

All sources and masks are embedded. No Assets folder is needed to open the project. Hide Typography for the clean artwork. Select an image thumbnail to transform it; select the mask thumbnail to refine its contour. The portrait's head remains in the source pixels behind its editable mask. The hidden untouched bouquet provides the original colors before edge cleanup.

User-supplied photographs:
Portrait: Assets/Portraits/Ladies - China/pexels-cottonbro-4973663.jpg
Bouquet: Assets/Nature/Bouquet/pexels-70588695-36325602.jpg
Old house: Assets/Towns - China/pexels-yifan-lai-1434608-31358263.jpg
The full 2333 × 3500 portrait and 6160 × 4640 bouquet are embedded. The house is embedded at 3000 × 3000. All scene imagery comes from these photographs; there are no generated photo layers.

The starting masks use local Apple Vision foreground selection. The portrait mask follows the garment neckline and removes the face. The flower mask has a slight inward edge correction and localized removal of pale lavender spill on the burgundy perimeter. Soft out-of-focus petals retain their photographed softness. This is a first design revision for visual review, not a claim that every edge is final.

Series typography follows Sylvan: Helvetica 36 px for the header/footer; Baskerville 70 px for the small title, 245 px for BLOOM and 36 px for page 02; Baskerville-Italic 96 px for the tagline. Header, title, tagline, footer and page origins follow the same grid.
