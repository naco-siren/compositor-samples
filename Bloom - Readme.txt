BLOOM / Botanical Portrait — Compositor Study 02

3200 × 2000 px / sRGB / 144 ppi / native format 11

A thick hollow frame made from an old-house photograph surrounds the red-and-yellow floral portrait. The warm rooftop background continues through its transparent opening. A red-and-yellow floral head replaces the portrait's head. Leaves, petals and patterned shawl overlap, retaining the original hand. The user composed the flowers, hand-refined their masks and painted the contact shading. Those source pixels, masks, placements and adjustment settings are preserved.

35 editable layers in eight folders. The project opens with Background A — Amber rooftops visible. Background B — Mountain village is an alternative; show only one background at a time. Both contain a complete embedded photograph, an editable fade mask, and clipped Hue/Saturation and Curves adjustments. The backgrounds extend across the page, retreat behind the flower silhouette and leave the title and footer clear. All typography remains editable.

The former rectangular house panel now forms a thick hollow frame in Subject > Frame — Old house. Its complete source photograph is retained behind an editable mask. The opening is transparent, so background A continues through it. Borders are approximately 230–245 document pixels; the flowers overlap the top border and the body extends past the bottom border. Frame adjustments have direct masks restricted to the ring, and an independent 17%-opacity shadow sits behind it. Hide Frame — Old house for the previous unframed layout. No Assets directory is required to open this project.
