BLOOM / Botanical Portrait — Compositor Study 02

3200 × 2000 px / sRGB / 144 ppi / native format 11

A red-and-yellow floral head replaces the portrait's head. Leaves, petals and patterned shawl overlap, retaining the original hand. The user composed the flowers, hand-refined their masks and painted the contact shading. Those source pixels, masks, placements and adjustment settings are preserved.

34 editable layers in eight folders. The project opens with Background A — Amber rooftops visible. Background B — Mountain village is an alternative; show only one background at a time. Both contain a complete embedded photograph, an editable fade mask, and clipped Hue/Saturation and Curves adjustments. The backgrounds extend across the page, retreat behind the flower silhouette and leave the title and footer clear. All typography remains editable.

The old rectangular house background remains intact in Subject > Setting, hidden. To restore it visually, hide Background A and Background B, then show Setting. For an exact rollback of the whole editable package, use the Git baseline listed below. No Assets directory is required to open this project.
