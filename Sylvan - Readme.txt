SYLVAN — A forest within.

Revised: enlarged canopy, posterior hair transition, clear face and neck.
3200 × 2000 px / sRGB

Open Sylvan - Double Exposure.comp in Compositor.
The 08 · TYPOGRAPHY folder toggles the poster copy on and off.
For a clean export, hide that folder and select a visible photo layer before using Export PNG.

The entire interior/exterior canopy uses one photograph, with identical pixels and transform for both native exposure passes. Leaf-shaped masks replace the posterior portrait outline and reveal the background through the foliage. The canopy is 25% larger and shifted left/down. The skin transition ends behind the cheek; the sparse trunk fades out before reaching the neck. Translucent frontal leaf remnants are removed. A separate face-light mask preserves the facial features.
